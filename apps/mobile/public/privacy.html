Noa on OpenMuse – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Noa on OpenMuse ("Noa") is a private, invite-only personal assistant. This policy explains what data Noa keeps and how it is used.

What we collect

  • Account: the anonymous user identifier Sign in with Apple provides, your email address if you choose to share it (it may be an Apple private relay address), and a display name.
  • Content you create: chat messages, tasks, goals, memories, files you upload, browser sessions and files in your agent's Linux computer.
  • Connected services you choose to link: Google (Gmail and Calendar) and, through Plaid, your bank account balances and transactions. Connection tokens are encrypted at rest. Bank access is read-only; Noa cannot move money.
  • Diagnostics: server logs needed to run and secure the service.

How it is used

Your data is used only to provide Noa's features to you. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with other users. Each account's data is kept separate.

Service providers

  • Anthropic processes your messages and the data the assistant reads to generate replies.
  • CopilotKit stores chat threads so conversations can be reopened.
  • Plaid connects to your bank when you link one.
  • Google when you connect Gmail or Calendar.
  • DigitalOcean hosts the server and database.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the app under Apps > Account > Delete account. This permanently removes your account, sessions, tasks, memories, files, browser data, computer workspace and connected-service tokens from our servers, and disconnects linked banks from Plaid.

Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. Connection tokens are encrypted with AES-256-GCM. Sessions are stored in the iOS Keychain on your device.

Children

Noa is not intended for children under 13.
